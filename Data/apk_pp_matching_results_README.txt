
This file documents the contents of the CSV file generated by the APK and Privacy Policy matching script.

Each row in the CSV corresponds to a single app_id and time (snapshot date). The key columns and their meanings are as follows:

- app_id: The unique identifier of the application (usually the package name).
- time: The time stamp or folder name representing the snapshot (e.g., "2023-09").
- causeOfFailure: The reason for failure, if any, in the matching or processing pipeline.
    Values and meanings:
    5 - Success: Both APK and privacy policy were matched and processed successfully.
    1 - Failed to download APK from apkmirror.
    2 - Failed to retrieve privacy policy from the Wayback Machine.
    3 - Failed during APK extraction with Androguard.
    4 - Failed during privacy policy extraction with PoliGraph.
- apk_permissions_categories_dataTypes_distribution: A dictionary (as string) representing the presence of data types (True/False/‘-’) as inferred from the APK’s declared permissions.
- privacy_policies_categories_dataTypes_distribution: A dictionary (as string) representing the presence of data types (True/False/‘-’) as inferred from the privacy policy text.

Note: A dash ('-') means at least one of the APK or the privacy policy is missing.
